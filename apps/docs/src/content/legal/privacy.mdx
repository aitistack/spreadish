# Privacy

This privacy policy covers the public Spreadish documentation site at [spreadish.aitistack.com](https://spreadish.aitistack.com) and the in-browser playground.

<Callout tone="tip" title="Short version">
    We do not run accounts on this site. The playground keeps workbook data in your browser. We do
    not sell personal data.
</Callout>

## Who we are

Spreadish is an open-source project published by Spreadish contributors under the AitiStack GitHub organization. The documentation site is hosted at `spreadish.aitistack.com`.

## What we collect

### Documentation site

The static docs site is designed to work without login. We do not operate a first-party user account system, newsletter signup, or advertising profile on this site.

Hosting, CDN, or analytics providers used to serve the site may process standard technical logs (for example IP address, user agent, and requested URL) as part of delivering pages. Those logs are controlled by the hosting provider’s policies.

### Playground

The playground stores workbook snapshots and related session data in your browser using IndexedDB (via `@spreadish/sometic`). That data stays on your device unless you export it or clear site storage yourself.

We do not sync playground workbooks to a Spreadish cloud account.

## Cookies and local storage

Essential storage may be used for:

- Theme preference in the playground
- Workbook / workspace persistence in IndexedDB
- Ordinary browser session behavior

We do not use advertising cookies on the documentation site.

## Third parties

When you leave this site for GitHub, npm, or other linked services, those services apply their own privacy policies.

## Children

The documentation is intended for developers. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact
